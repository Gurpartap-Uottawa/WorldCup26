# World Cup 2026 Screenings

Helps fans find physical places to watch World Cup 2026 matches with other people.

## Language

### Screenings

**Screening**:
A physical location where you can watch a match. It is a place, not tied to any one match, and says nothing about cost.
_Avoid_: Venue (that means the stadium), watch spot, viewing location, free screening

**Fan Festival**:
A Screening run officially by FIFA in a host city, showing every match of the tournament.
_Avoid_: Fan zone, fan fest, watch zone

**Curated Screening**:
A Screening that was hand-picked and checked by the maintainer, such as a park or a soccer bar.
_Avoid_: Popular watch spot, watch spot

**Screening Lead**:
An unverified web search result that might point to a Screening. It is not a Screening itself and has no location.
_Avoid_: Search result screening, web screening
